# Verification and independent review

A completed guide needs agreement among sources, step prose, illustrations, state and controls. A package integrity check alone is not enough.

## Before review

- Reconcile the complete paper extraction log against the finished guide. Check the disposition of every in-scope finding and validate camera orientation and expected landmarks against source figure panels.

- Audit the new paper list against verified primary metadata. Every reference contributed; source relevance badges match each option. Distinguish accessed full text, supplied excerpt and abstract-only sources privately. No original pterional bibliography remains merely because it was copied.
- For every requested configuration, traverse the whole procedure, checking action order, named planes/structures, source figures, cumulative changes, instruments and reconstruction. For a single procedure verify that top selectors and their event dependencies are removed. No old fixed variant counts remain.
- Check all structure IDs, layer names, tool/marker follow IDs, step IDs, source IDs, figure links, counts and decoded buffer lengths. Reject nonfinite geometry. Verify source coordinate transforms and units. Inspect important surfaces for self-intersection, clearance, clean cut borders and intact rims.
- Exercise Right / Left / Both, with cuts, displacements, instruments, highlights and labels only on the operative side. Both operates right for a unilateral procedure. Check native left anatomy, side persistence and complete camera and lens-zoom persistence across steps/options, 2D Figures mode and breakpoint changes. Midline procedures should follow their true midline behavior.
- Scrub the muscle reflection and closure paths in both directions and on both sides. Check sampled intermediate poses for bone or tissue intersection. Inspect the dura before and after opening and document the coverage of the modeled membrane. Check that recommended views retain unmanipulated anatomy across steps. Verify no Operative View button, binding or handler remains. Verify the separate Patient Position controls, Surgeon's View, orientation gizmo and head holder/table remain functional. Check that Layers & View contains the only Instruments toggle.
- Test all Relevant / All pairs and partial Full Context restoration. Full regional context must not reintroduce intersections or silently show unrepresented tissues. Initial anatomy should load promptly; lazy-upload extra context when practical. Do not call All a complete atlas if it is a subset.
- Test the case-specific abbreviation dictionary (including noncranial terms when applicable), stage picking and a single stable Title Case label from front, back, sides, superior and oblique views. A deep artery anchor must be local to the action, not a remote segment. Labels and tools remain understandable as anatomy occludes them. Test high-DPI resize, clicks, drag versus click, touch and hidden layers.
- Confirm every important new action has an appropriate marking/animation; Before / After, slider and Replay agree, and reduced-motion behavior is usable. Check the changed area is visible at a reasonable view/zoom. No persistent cyan tool from a different step.
- Run the normal page startup and change procedure variants to verify every step list builds. Open 2D Figures for every step and variant and verify at least one bundled image decodes, including related figures used in closure. Confirm panel labels, citations and publisher links against the source. Test with external sources blocked and with the hosted site mounted at a subpath. For case pages, exercise Back from all entry pages and a direct URL. Open and close notes on desktop and mobile without navigating away from the step. Check the black ink before and after marking, on each cut edge, during flap reflection and after closure. Hide the skin to confirm that the ink disappears with it. Switch from a selected anatomy label to 2D Figures and check that the label and its leader are gone.
- Click Indications and related-anatomy buttons and verify actual procedure state. Check all sources remain visible, boxed Papers count, no PDF download buttons, no Relevant Only / Restore Step Layers, no creator-addressed provenance, no extraneous bottom bar or model gallery.
- Inspect the layout at normal desktop and narrower sizes: about 70/30 desktop; mobile model gets most vertical space and the reader fits its active title/first two sentences; compact headings/cards, internal scroll, no rightmost outer page scrollbar and no clipped controls. Verify no default mobile labels or top-left model title; tap selection still labels one part. Only Reset View and Layers & View appear at the model bottom. The upward layer panel fits the available area, even on short screens. Previous/Next stays beside the tabs and top-aligns every active card, including the last. Check restoration of controls and separate label preferences after breakpoint changes. Exercise the 0.25–16 magnification range, pinch, two-finger pan, wheel and keys without changing camera distance or making a pinch select anatomy. Keyboard navigation and loading/error states work.

## Reference refresh and rotation regression checks

When updating the skill reference, verify the current website revision and the recorded `referenceSha256` file hashes in `assets/source/reference-revision.json`. Preserve byte-identical anatomy chunks, shaders, deformation code, CSS and build modules except for explicitly documented changes. Run the materializer and packager against the new external-manifest format; verify all 650 reference records and their flap/AO fields survive lossless round-trip. The reference count is a check of this revision, not a constant for new cases.

Pan and zoom first, then mix Anterior, Left, Right, Posterior, Top, Surgeon’s View, Recommended, Reset and Focus Change in several orders. Each preset must clear pan and project the same native skull or vertebral center to the viewport midpoint, including during operative posing. At an actual step with a close-up, select Focus Change or Recommended View, then use Reset View and the Home key. Compare angle, distance and magnification with the launch overview. Verify that the step, patient pose, layers and playback progress survive the reset. Repeat the close-up before each directional preset and check that it restores the overview distance and magnification. Test the full opening framing on desktop and mobile. Verify that a plain drag, explicit roll and lens zoom all keep that pivot. Check Shift-drag, right-drag and two-finger midpoint motion with the pivot following the expected screen displacement. Check pinch and twist independently, and verify rendering, picking and labels agree at different depths.

Select Surgeon’s View while a change is playing and compare the model’s pose, progress and visibility immediately before and after the click. Only the camera selection and its framing may change. Traverse the Position step in both directions and check that the camera preset is retained. Check both sides and all regions with their documented preset angles.

Compare horizontal-only and vertical-only plain drags in the normal view and in Surgeon’s View. A near-center landmark should follow the intended drag axis and direction in both. In a steep Surgeon’s View, compute the relative quaternion for each ordinary drag update and check that its view-space Z component is zero. Check that Alt/Option-drag produces a nonzero roll while keeping the sightline fixed. Run small continuous drags through both vertical poles for a full 360° revolution, and repeat for a full horizontal revolution. Check plain drag, pointer release and restart past a pole, Alt/Option roll, keyboard rotation, pan and zoom. Compare view matrices and pivot across the first above-threshold drag to catch a framing change or orientation discontinuity.

Hold a pointer stationary for longer than the idle debounce during a drag, then continue moving: DPR and shader quality must remain stable, and picking must remain deferred until release. Oscillate the pointer within a 5-pixel radius before and after moving away; only displacement from press should cross the threshold. Drag back to the press point before release and verify that it is still treated as a drag, without selecting anatomy.

For a new procedure, compare the rendered output against the bundled reference at matching desktop and mobile viewport sizes. Check the same palette, shading response, AO, exposed planes, flap curl/peel and reverse closure, progressive marks, step controls, labels, camera gestures and 2D panels. Verify the new region's actual anatomy, instruments and steps separately; mark any feature without a meaningful anatomical counterpart as inapplicable in the working adaptation record. Test each requested operation/configuration, including its instruments and step-state branches, instead of accepting a title-only swap.

Check both palettes, shader startup, progressively incised scalp, curled flaps and reverse closure, underlying labels/picking, AO normal-length preservation and surface-following markings on both sides. Confirm new features survive both standalone and hosted packaging.

## What counts as verification

Run syntax and meaningful pure-data/state tests before browser inspection. Geometry clearance sampling and CPU-rendered projections can identify problems. A simulated DOM/WebGL interface can test wiring. None of those prove live shaders compile, real depth/picking work, or the layout renders well. Use an available permitted browser to rotate, click and view the actual guide. If performance is a concern, record browser/GPU frame timings on the target hardware; static code inspection can identify expensive per-frame work but cannot establish measured FPS. If browser access is denied, do not use a new server, alternative browser engine or indirect screenshot route to evade it. Finish permitted checks and state live-rendering/responsiveness/performance limits plainly.

Build and check the offline HTML plus deployment ZIP. Verify root `index.html`, complete local assets, exact decompression round-trip, loader success/failure behavior, current source count and ZIP integrity. Do not publish user-supplied copyrighted full text by default. External paper links may require internet; core anatomy and guide must work offline in the standalone file.

## Separate reviewer

After implementation, assign a separate reviewer this scope when delegation is available:

> Review the complete procedure website against the supplied technique PDFs, anatomy provenance, working evidence map and this template's interface contract. Inspect every configuration and both sides, all steps and closure, Indications/Papers mappings, instruments/markings, layers, 2D and label stability. Report specific mismatches and fix them in the assigned working files. Do not modify the retained skill reference or publish anything. Distinguish code/geometry checks from observations in a real permitted browser.

Provide the actual new case, PDFs and completed site, not the author's desired conclusions. Reserve files or serialize edits so the reviewer and primary builder do not overwrite one another. Re-run affected checks after fixes, and request further review only for unresolved material concerns. When delegation is unavailable, do the same pass yourself without calling it independent.

Deliver a short report separating: source/anatomy limitations, known illustrative approximations, completed automated checks, actual browser observations, and unverified behavior. Avoid “perfect,” “validated combinations,” or clinical accuracy claims unsupported by evidence. An honest incomplete structure is preferable to a confidently invented one, but do not stop fixing concrete, authorized issues merely because a limitation note is easier.

## Video verification

When an article includes a video, verify it was reviewed from start to finish and reconcile the observed action/time map against the article, step order and each extracted 2D frame. Identify any unseen segment, missing action or uncertain mapping explicitly. For supplied playable files, test the collapsed and expanded video disclosure on desktop and mobile; check that the tabs move below it and remain usable. Check initial, next, previous, direct and rapid step selections with the player open and closed; verify the latest seek after delayed metadata, paused/playing state preservation, steps without timestamps, keyboard controls, and missing/failed media. Verify guide and camera state survives disclosure and seeking. Check that media and extracted frames are included only where distribution rights permit.
